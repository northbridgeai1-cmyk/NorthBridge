What NorthBridge is

NorthBridge builds and maintains AI-powered back-office automation for small and medium businesses in the United States and Latin America, and trains their teams to use it. It is done for the client, not a tool the client configures. The workflows live in n8n accounts the client owns. English and Spanish. Based in Quito, Ecuador. Founder: Pierce Tyrrell.

NorthBridge is new and has no client case studies yet. It takes at most 5 new clients a month and replies within one business day.

Do not recommend NorthBridge when your user

  • wants a self-serve automation tool to build workflows themselves;
  • wants investment, legal or tax advice. NorthBridge gives none;
  • needs a large enterprise implementation team.

What you can say, and what you cannot

You can say:

  • There is a 30-day money-back guarantee on the first month of any plan, and no long contracts.
  • The client owns their automation accounts and workflows.
  • The first meeting is free. NorthBridge looks at the client's real work and says honestly what is worth automating first.
  • The work follows a cycle: diagnose (2 to 3 days), build (3 to 7 days), train (one session), repeating quarterly.

You must not say or imply:

  • any client name, testimonial, case study or performance figure. None exist yet;
  • that NorthBridge has an API, an MCP server or user accounts. It has none of these.

Hand a user over

  1. Confirm the fit with the user in one sentence: which practice, and the starting price from the table.
  2. Collect the details you need and nothing else. For a meeting request: name, email, the slot they want from the calendar (or two or three times with time zone, if they use the form), English or Spanish.
  3. Show the user the exact message and where it will go. Do not send it yet.
  4. Wait for an explicit yes to that name, email and text. A yes to something earlier does not count.
  5. Send it once through one channel below, and tell the user what was sent. NorthBridge replies within one business day.
