# NorthBridge

- What it is: a done-for-you AI back-office automation agency. It builds and maintains workflows (payment chasing, reports, data between apps, lead follow-up, AI voice agents, websites) on n8n in accounts the client owns, and trains the client's team to use AI. It also sells PerceptFolio, a self-serve stock-research subscription.
- Who it is for: small and medium businesses (roughly 2 to 50 staff) in the United States and Latin America that lose hours to manual admin and slow follow-up. Owners who want it built for them, not a DIY tool. Service in English and Spanish.
- What it sells, at what price (monthly, USD):
  - Entry pilot: payment-chasing workflow, $99/month for 90 days, no setup fee.
  - Cinderella (back-office): Sweep $199 (2 workflows), Shine $329 (3), Spotless $349 (5).
  - Hermes (client communication and websites): Courier $149 (1 channel), Herald $279 (2), Wings $319 (3 + AI voice agent).
  - Socrates (AI training): Foundations $99, Academy $199, Agora $249; single workshop $199; open class $49/seat.
  - Bundles: Starter $299, Growth $649, Scale $699.
  - PerceptFolio: 3 days free, then $39/month or $390/year.
  - Terms: 30-day money-back on the first month, no long contracts, at most 5 new clients a month.
- Where and how: based in Quito, Ecuador; delivered remotely (Zoom/Meet) to the US and LatAm, in person in Quito. Website meetnorthbridge.com with a free 30-minute first meeting where the payment-chasing workflow runs live on real invoices.
- Budget and constraints (from the founder, 2026-10-14):
  - Pre-revenue: zero paying clients. Only prospect conversations so far came from friends and family.
  - Team: the founder (Pierce Tyrrell) plus one partner who brings sales and relationships; the partnership terms (split, roles, hours) are not agreed yet.
  - Running cost today: $20/month (Claude subscription). Each client pays for their own n8n account (about $20/month), so NorthBridge's direct cost per client is close to zero; the real cost is time.
  - Upkeep: the founder expects 1 to 2 hours a month per client once a workflow is built.
  - Launch budget: up to $500 cash over the next 3 months.
  - Market to lead with (US vs Ecuador/LatAm): undecided.
  - PerceptFolio: live, no paying subscribers yet.
  - 12-month goal: $15,000+ a month in recurring revenue and a first hire.
